Mission Name: IKE Solutions TikTok Revenue Engine
Mission Owner: Isiah Tarik Howard
Lead Orchestrator: Hermes

Initial Execution Window: Thirty days
Primary Objective: Convert TikTok attention into measurable revenue from educational products, workshops, affiliate offers, sponsorships, and qualified institutional leads.

## 1. EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVE

Build and deploy a lawful, evidence-based TikTok monetization system for IKE Solutions.

The system shall not depend solely on TikTok creator payouts. It shall use TikTok as the top of a broader commercial funnel:

TikTok Content
      ↓
Audience Trust
      ↓
Lead Capture
      ↓
Low-Cost Educational Product
      ↓
Paid Workshop or Premium Toolkit
      ↓
Membership, Consulting Intake, or Institutional Pilot

The system must distinguish between revenue controlled by TikTok, third-party sponsors/sellers, and IKE Solutions directly. Priority shall be given to revenue sources that IKE Solutions owns and controls.

## 2. MISSION SUCCESS DEFINITION

The mission is successful when all of the following exist:

- A verified TikTok monetization eligibility report.

- A minimum thirty-day content calendar.
- At least twenty completed short-form video scripts.

- One entry-level digital product.

- One paid workshop offer.

- A TikTok Shop affiliate shortlist.

A high number of views without captured leads, product sales, qualified inquiries, or platform eligibility progress shall not be classified as mission success.

## 3. OPERATING PRINCIPLES

### 3.1 Evidence before claims
No legal, financial, tax, credit, trust, UCC, debt, or court claim may be published without a source, a jurisdiction check, a date check, a plain-language explanation, a risk classification, and human approval where required.

### 3.2 Education, not guaranteed outcomes
Content shall not promise: guaranteed debt elimination, guaranteed credit deletion, guaranteed court victory, guaranteed UCC priority, guaranteed access to Treasury accounts, guaranteed acceptance of any document, guaranteed financial return, or a specific legal result without individualized review.

### 3.3 Original content
Videos intended for TikTok Creator Rewards shall be original, high quality, and generally at least one minute long. Sponsored material, Duets, and Stitches should not be assumed to qualify for Creator Rewards. Eligibility must be confirmed inside the current TikTok account before agents classify content as reward-eligible.

### 3.4 Platform terms are changeable
TikTok eligibility, payout, affiliate, and project rules may change. Agents must treat the current account interface and current TikTok policy pages as controlling.

### 3.5 Revenue ownership first
Agents shall prioritize in this order:
1. IKE Solutions digital products.
2. Paid workshops.
3. Institutional training and SintraPrime pilots.
4. TikTok Series.
5. Relevant affiliate commissions.
6. Sponsorships.
7. Subscriptions and Gifts.
8. Creator Rewards.

## 7. WORKSTREAM ONE — BRAND POSITIONING AND SAFETY FRAMEWORK

Owner: Sentinel
Supporting Agents: Athena, Justice Scribe
Deadline: Days one through three

### 7.1 Core brand promise
IKE Solutions teaches consumers how to identify the governing rules, organize evidence, challenge unsupported claims, and choose lawful escalation options.

### 7.2 Prohibited positioning
Do not position the account as a secret debt-discharge system, Treasury-account access service, guaranteed credit-repair company, court-escape system, provider of individualized legal representation, source of guaranteed financial instruments, method for placing unauthorized liens, or replacement for licensed legal or tax counsel.

### 7.3 Content classification system
Every legal or commercial claim shall receive one classification:
- V1 — Verified primary authority
- V2 — Supported secondary interpretation
- V3 — Jurisdiction-dependent
- V4 — Disputed or unresolved theory
- V5 — Unsupported, misleading, or false

### 7.4 Risk rating system
Each script shall receive: LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, or PROHIBITED.

**LOW:** General document organization; evidence preservation; reading account statements; public agency complaint procedures; plain-language definitions.

**MODERATE:** Statute explanations; credit-report disputes; debt-validation procedures; contract analysis; UCC explanations.

**HIGH:** Litigation strategy; tax forms; UCC filings against named parties; affidavits accusing fraud; advice to stop making payments; claims involving negotiable instruments; trust asset protection; bankruptcy; court jurisdiction.

**PROHIBITED:** Instructions to submit false tax information; unauthorized liens; forged or altered instruments; deceptive payment instruments; claims that a billing statement is automatically legal tender; guaranteed secret-account access; impersonation; threats unsupported by law.

### 7.5 Standard educational disclaimer
Use where appropriate: "This content provides general consumer education, not individualized legal, tax, or financial advice. Outcomes depend on the governing contract, jurisdiction, dates, parties, and evidence." Do not use the disclaimer as permission to publish otherwise misleading material.

### 7.6 Required artifacts
- `governance/tiktok/TIKTOK_CONTENT_SAFETY_STANDARD.md`
- `governance/tiktok/TIKTOK_CLAIM_CLASSIFICATION_MATRIX.md`
- `governance/tiktok/TIKTOK_ADVERTISING_DISCLOSURE_STANDARD.md`
- `governance/tiktok/TIKTOK_HUMAN_REVIEW_TRIGGER_LIST.md`

### 7.7 Gate G-1
PASS only if the prohibited-claim list exists, the disclaimer standard exists, human-review triggers are defined, the classification matrix is operational, and every publishing agent can apply the framework.
