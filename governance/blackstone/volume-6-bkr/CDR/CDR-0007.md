# CDR-0007 — Track Constitutional Lineage via BKR Extension Rather Than a Parallel Registry

## Problem

BKGC v1.0 (the original "Enterprise Knowledge Governance Framework") was recovered
from a ChatGPT share thread and archived at `volume-1-bkgc/archive/BKGC_v1.0.md`.
A request followed to strengthen that archival with cryptographic integrity
(SHA-256), provenance metadata, and a registry tracking version/status/
supersession lineage for BKGC and, prospectively, other constitutional
documents across the wider Blackstone ecosystem (e.g., SEOS, an Engineering
Constitution) that do not yet exist in this repository.

## Alternatives Considered

1. **Create a new top-level `governance/registry/` tree** (with its own
   `schemas/`, `constitutions/`, and `lineage/` subfolders) as a general
   ecosystem-wide Governance Registry. Rejected: this is precisely the
   "parallel top-level registry... outside BKR" that
   `ARCHITECTURAL_FREEZE_NOTICE.md` §5 prohibits without an architecture
   revision, and it would also introduce a governance surface for
   constitutions (SEOS, Engineering, Mission Control, Trust, Automation) that
   have not been authored or ratified anywhere in this repository.
2. **Fabricate placeholder constitutional content** for the not-yet-existing
   ecosystem constitutions so the registry would have something to reference.
   Rejected: BKGC Article I and Article XVI prohibit inventing content; a
   registry MUST NOT imply the existence of governance text that has not been
   drafted or ratified.
3. **Do nothing beyond the archival itself.** Rejected: it leaves BKGC v1.0
   without integrity verification, provenance, or a queryable lineage record,
   weakening the audit trail the Blackstone Governance Library otherwise
   requires (BKGC Art. VII, Art. X, Art. XIX).
4. **Extend the existing Blackstone Knowledge Registry (BKR) with a
   constitution-lineage registry, and add SHA-256 plus provenance metadata
   directly to the archived document.** Selected: `ARCHITECTURAL_FREEZE_NOTICE.md`
   §4 explicitly permits "adding new registries under BKR" as not requiring
   architecture revision, since "BKR is the canonical registry volume; new
   registries extend it rather than parallel it." This satisfies the
   underlying need (lineage, integrity, provenance, forward-looking
   placeholders) without violating the freeze.

## Decision

1. `volume-1-bkgc/archive/BKGC_v1.0.md` front matter is extended with
   `sha256`, `sha256_scope`, `recovery_method`, `recovered_by`, `reviewer`,
   `verification_status`, and a back-reference to this CDR and to the new
   registry entry.
2. A new registry, `volume-6-bkr/REGISTRIES/constitution_registry.md`, is
   added under BKR to track constitutional documents: identifier, title,
   version, status, effective date, supersession lineage, SHA-256, governing
   CDR, evidence reference, verification status, owner, and repository path.
3. The registry includes rows for the seven Blackstone Governance Library
   volumes (cross-referencing each volume's own `CHANGELOG.md` as the
   authoritative version history) and rows for named ecosystem constitutions
   mentioned in project discussion (SEOS, Engineering, Mission Control,
   Trust, Automation), each marked `status: planned` with no populated
   content, version, or hash fields.
4. `volume-6-bkr/BKR.md` § 1.1 is updated to list the new registry.
5. Promoting a `planned` row to `draft` or `active` REQUIRES that the
   corresponding constitutional document actually exist in the repository
   and that a CDR record the promotion; this registry itself never
   originates constitutional content.

## Governing Principles

- `ARCHITECTURAL_FREEZE_NOTICE.md` §4 — new registries extend BKR rather than
  paralleling it.
- BKGC Article I, Article XVI — no fabrication of evidence, citations, or
  (by extension) governance content.
- BKGC Article VII, Article X — provenance and evidence-integrity
  requirements.
- BKGC Article XIX — authority lifecycle metadata (effective date, amendment
  history, successor authority, historical archive reference).

## Expected Consequences

- BKGC v1.0's archived text now carries a verifiable integrity hash and
  provenance trail.
- Future constitutional documents (ratified or planned) have a single,
  compliant place to be registered without repeated architecture debates.
- The seven-volume architecture and the "BKR is the canonical registry"
  rule remain intact; no eighth volume or parallel registry was introduced.

## Revisit Conditions

If the number or complexity of ecosystem-wide (non-library) constitutions
grows to the point that a dedicated cross-cutting registry volume is
operationally justified, revisit through the GB-1 review trigger or a
Constitutional Amendment per `volume-1-bkgc/RATIFICATION.md`, per
`ARCHITECTURAL_FREEZE_NOTICE.md` §5.
