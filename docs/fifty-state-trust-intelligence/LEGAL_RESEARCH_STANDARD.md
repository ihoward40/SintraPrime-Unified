# Legal Research Standard

Phase 1 legal research is source-first and review-gated.

- Prefer primary New Jersey authority: enacted statutes, official regulations, court rules, and binding decisions.
- Official agency guidance may explain filing-office practice but cannot override statutes or regulations.
- Secondary and private materials may locate issues but cannot support production rules alone.
- Every rule must cite at least one authority record and carry limitations.
- `PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFIED` verifies the source location only. It is not a legal opinion or professional approval.
- Missing effective dates, conflicts, supersession uncertainty, or unsupported private claims require human review.

New Jersey Phase 1 sources include official New Jersey Legislature session laws for the Uniform Trust Code, UVTA, and Article 9, plus DORES UCC guidance and fee materials. N.J.A.C. 17:33 is located but remains limited until checked against an official New Jersey Administrative Code source.
