# Case — Conflicting Statutes

## Case ID

BGC-CASE-010

## Title

Conflicting Statutes

## Scenario

A federal statute and a state statute impose different requirements on the same activity. The user asks which rule applies.

## Inputs

- Federal statute.
- State statute.
- Relevant federal preemption doctrine cases.
- State attorney general guidance.

## Governing Principles and Articles

- BKGC § VIII — Authority Governance
- BKGC-R-006 Controlling Authority Binding
- BKC-C-004 Authority
- BGS-S-011 Review Levels

## Evidence Evaluation

| Evidence | Source Category | Weight | Notes |
|----------|-----------------|--------|-------|
| Federal statute | Government Publication | Controlling (federal scope) | Supremacy Clause applies |
| State statute | Government Publication | Controlling (state scope) | Potentially preempted |
| Preemption cases | Judicial Decision | Controlling | Define when federal law preempts |
| AG guidance | Government Guidance | Persuasive | Non-binding interpretation |

## Reasoning

1. Federal controlling authority may preempt state law under the Supremacy Clause and relevant case law.
2. The preemption analysis itself is a legal question governed by judicial precedent.
3. The state statute remains valid where not preempted.
4. The ecosystem must present the preemption framework and preserve both statutes.

## Outcome

- Cite federal statute as potentially controlling.
- Cite state statute as controlling unless preempted.
- Reference preemption doctrine cases.
- Flag that the question requires legal analysis to determine whether preemption applies.
- Escalate to R2 human review if the conclusion will be used operationally.
