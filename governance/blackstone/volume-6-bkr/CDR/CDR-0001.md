# CDR-0001 — Adopt the Blackstone Governance Library

## Problem

SintraPrime-Unified makes consequential decisions using AI agents, legal sources, and operational evidence. Without a durable governance framework, terminology, authority, and review standards drift across sessions, agents, and implementations.

## Alternatives Considered

1. **Ad-hoc governance notes.** Rejected because they lack traceability, versioning, and enforcement.
2. **Single monolithic policy document.** Rejected because it would mix enduring principles with operational details that must evolve quickly.
3. **Seven-volume constitutional library.** Selected because it separates constitutional stability from engineering agility and provides a clear dependency hierarchy.

## Decision

Adopt the Blackstone Governance Library with volumes:

1. BKGC — Constitution
2. BGS — Standards
3. BKC — Knowledge Core
4. BRA — Reference Architecture
5. BCCM — Certification & Compliance
6. BKR — Knowledge Registry
7. BGC — Governance Casebook

## Governing Principles

- BKGC § 2.1 Separation of Methodology from Conclusion
- BKGC § 3.1 Governed Knowledge Doctrine
- BKGC § 27.1 Platform Neutrality

## Expected Consequences

- Terminology is standardized across agents and code.
- Authority conflicts are handled by explicit rules rather than agent discretion.
- Major governance decisions are preserved as CDRs.
- Certification is based on demonstrated compliance, not model identity.

## Revisit Conditions

When the ecosystem expands beyond SintraPrime-Unified or the constitutional hierarchy proves insufficient, revisit as a constitutional amendment.
