# DRS-001 — Decision Record Standard

## Purpose

Define the required structure for durable Howard Life Institution decision records so decisions remain auditable, reproducible, and reviewable over time.

## Scope

This standard governs every institutional decision record (`DR-xxxx`) entered into the Decision Register.
All required sections in DRS-001 apply to `Draft`, `Proposed`, `Ratified`, `Rejected`, `Superseded`, and `Archived` records. For `Draft` and `Proposed`, ratification fields remain pending until a decision-authority sign-off occurs.

## Normative Requirements

1. Every decision record MUST have a unique `Decision ID` in the form `DR-YYYY-NNNN`, where `YYYY` is the four-digit calendar year of record creation and `NNNN` is a zero-padded sequence unique within that year.
2. Every decision record MUST include the mandatory fields in this standard.
3. Every decision record MUST identify who holds decision authority and who authored the record.
4. Every decision record MUST distinguish accepted evidence from rejected evidence.
5. Every decision record MUST preserve reasoning, alternatives, and consequences.
6. Every decision record MUST include status metadata and supersession metadata where applicable to maintain history.
7. Decision records MUST preserve an immutable ratified decision body; post-ratification corrections or reversals MUST be captured by a new decision record that references the prior record.

## Required Structure

Each decision record MUST include, at minimum, the following sections and fields:

| Section | Required Fields |
|---|---|
| Header | Decision ID, Title, Status, Effective Date (`TBD` allowed pre-ratification), Decision Authority, Record Author |
| Decision Context | Question, Trigger/Event, Decision Scope |
| Decision Outcome | Final Decision, Rationale, Consequences |
| Alternatives | Alternatives Considered, Rejection Reason per alternative |
| Evidence | Accepted Evidence, Rejected Evidence, Evidence Quality/Confidence |
| Governance | Constraints/Policies Considered, Risks, Required Safeguards |
| Execution | Implementation Owner, Immediate Actions, Follow-up Actions |
| Lifecycle | Review Date, Supersedes (`None` when not applicable), Superseded By (`None` when not applicable), Lifecycle Notes, Lifecycle Action Log, Change Log (initialized at record creation) |
| Sign-off | Ratification Entries (Role, Ratifier, Ratification Date, Ratification Decision) |

## Status Model

Decision records MUST use one of the following statuses:

- `Draft`
- `Proposed`
- `Ratified`
- `Rejected`
- `Superseded`
- `Archived` (ratified records only)

Allowed initial workflow transitions:

- `Draft -> Proposed`
- `Proposed -> Ratified`
- `Proposed -> Rejected`

Allowed post-ratification transitions:

- `Ratified -> Superseded`
- `Ratified -> Archived`

## Compliance Rule

All `DR-` entries in the Decision Register are official governed records under DRS-001 from creation time.
A decision is institutionally durable only when all of the following are true:

1. `Status` is `Ratified`, `Superseded`, or `Archived`.
2. The decision-authority sign-off includes `Ratification Decision: RATIFY`.

`Draft` and `Proposed` records remain official Decision Register entries, but are pre-durability states until ratification.
`Rejected` records are official non-durable terminal entries.

## Supersession Reference Format

- `Supersedes` and `Superseded By` fields MUST reference decision IDs in `DR-YYYY-NNNN` format.
- Multiple references MAY be listed as a bullet list of decision IDs.
- When not applicable, fields MUST be set to `None`.

## Rejected Record Conventions

For records with `Status: Rejected`:

- `Review Date` SHOULD be `None`.
- `Supersedes` SHOULD be `None`.
- `Superseded By` SHOULD be `None`.
- Ratification table MUST include a `Ratification Decision` value of `REJECT`.
- `Rejected` status is only valid for a `Proposed -> Rejected` transition after decision-authority sign-off.

## Lifecycle Clarification

- `Change Log` entries are for drafting edits before ratification only.
- The `Ratification` section is finalized at the `Proposed -> Ratified` or `Proposed -> Rejected` transition.
- After `Ratified`, the decision body sections (`Question` through `Follow-up Actions`) are immutable.
- When `Superseded By` is populated, predecessor `Status` MUST be updated to `Superseded`.
- `Superseded By` is optional until a successor decision exists; once superseded, predecessor records receive this field as an allowed post-ratification lifecycle metadata update.
- Post-ratification lifecycle metadata updates are limited to `Status`, `Review Date`, `Supersedes`, `Superseded By`, `Lifecycle Notes`, and append-only `Lifecycle Action Log` entries that record authority and date for supersession or archival actions.
- Any substantive change to the decision itself requires a new `DR-` entry linked through `Supersedes` and `Superseded By`.
