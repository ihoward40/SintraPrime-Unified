# Decision Register — DOX Contract

## Purpose

Own standards, templates, and register assets for durable institutional decisions in BOE.

## Ownership

- Repository owner (Isiah Howard) holds decision-ratification authority.
- Contributors may draft and maintain standards and records in this subtree.

## Local Contracts

- `DRS-001.md` defines the mandatory structure for institutional decision records.
- Decision records in this subtree MUST use `DR-YYYY-NNNN` identifiers.
- Ratified decision records are immutable and supersession is handled by linked successor records.

## Work Guidance

- Keep standards explicit, auditable, and implementation-neutral.
- Ensure templates match normative requirements exactly.
