# TikTok Content Safety Standard

Mission: SP-TKM-001
Owner: Sentinel
Effective Date: 2026-07-27

## 1. Brand Promise

IKE Solutions teaches consumers how to identify governing rules, organize evidence, challenge unsupported claims, and choose lawful escalation options.

Supporting statement:

> We help consumers turn scattered records into organized evidence they can understand, preserve, and use when communicating with companies, regulators, or qualified professionals.

## 2. Permitted Positioning

- General consumer-law education
- Document organization and evidence preservation
- Plain-language definitions of legal, financial, and credit terms
- Public agency complaint procedures
- How to request records, validate debts, dispute credit reports, and redact safely
- How to prepare for a qualified professional review

## 3. Prohibited Positioning

Do not market or describe IKE Solutions as:

- A law firm or provider of legal representation
- A debt-elimination service
- A guaranteed credit-repair service
- A secret Treasury-account service
- A provider of guaranteed legal outcomes
- A method for evading valid debts, judgments, taxes, or court obligations
- A source of unauthorized liens or UCC filings against named parties
- A replacement for licensed legal, tax, or financial counsel

## 4. Prohibited Claims and Instructions

Stop publication immediately if content contains any of the following:

- Guaranteed debt discharge, cancellation, or removal
- Guaranteed credit-score increase or deletion of accurate items
- Guaranteed court victory or jurisdiction escape
- Instructions to submit false tax information or forged instruments
- Claims that a billing statement or coupon is automatically legal tender
- Claims that a UCC filing alone creates rights that do not otherwise exist
- Advice to ignore a court date or stop making payments without individualized review
- Accusations of fraud against named individuals or companies without verified evidence
- Promises of access to secret government accounts or Treasury direct accounts
- Testimonials presented as typical or guaranteed results

## 5. Standard Educational Disclaimer

Use this or a substantially similar disclaimer on every video that discusses legal, credit, debt, tax, or court topics:

> This content provides general consumer education, not individualized legal, tax, or financial advice. Outcomes depend on the governing contract, jurisdiction, dates, parties, and evidence.

Do not use the disclaimer to justify otherwise misleading material.

## 6. Publication Checklist

Before any content is published, Dispatch Marshal must confirm:

- [ ] Final script approved by Justice Scribe
- [ ] Athena verified moderate- or high-risk claims
- [ ] Sentinel assigned risk rating and approved
- [ ] Sources recorded for V1–V3 claims
- [ ] Standard disclaimer present
- [ ] Personal data redacted
- [ ] No copyrighted music unless licensed
- [ ] Commercial or affiliate content labeled
- [ ] CTA link tested and includes UTM parameters
- [ ] Pinned comment prepared
- [ ] Cover text readable on mobile

## 7. Commercial and Affiliate Disclosure

- Affiliate content must include a clear verbal and on-screen disclosure such as: "I may earn a commission if you purchase through this link."
- Sponsored content must use TikTok's branded-content tools and include a clear disclosure.
- Do not imply a product is legally required unless it is.
- Do not promote products solely for commission if they are unrelated to evidence organization.

## 8. Stop Conditions

Publication halts immediately when:

- A material legal claim cannot be verified.
- A source is misquoted or fabricated.
- A consumer's identity is exposed.
- A sponsor requests deception.
- A product has a serious safety or fraud concern.
- TikTok issues a policy warning related to the content.
- A video implies guaranteed debt discharge or secret-account access.
- A tax form is discussed as a payment device without verified lawful basis.
- A UCC filing is presented as creating rights that do not otherwise exist.
- A court deadline could be jeopardized by general advice.

## 9. Human Review Triggers

Mandatory human review for:

- Legal interpretations
- Individual case analysis
- Sponsor contracts
- TikTok Series pricing
- Product refund terms
- Tax content
- Court-content scripts
- UCC filing instructions
- Debt-payment recommendations
- Claims about specific companies
- Testimonials
- Content involving children or private customer records
- Any HIGH-risk classification
