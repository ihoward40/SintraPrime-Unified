# Refund Policy Recommendation — IKE Solutions Consumer Evidence Products

Mission: SP-TKM-001
Owner: Revenue Architect
Status: Internal draft — not yet published

## Digital Products

Applies to: Consumer Evidence Intake Pack, Consumer Evidence Starter Kit

### 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

Buyers may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase if:

- The files were never downloaded, or
- The files are materially defective or misdescribed.

### After Download

Once files have been downloaded and opened, refunds are at the sole discretion of IKE Solutions. We may offer a refund if the product does not match the description or if technical issues prevent access.

### Process

1. Email ISIAHH@ikesolutions.org from the email address used at purchase.
2. Include order confirmation or transaction reference.
3. State the reason for the refund request.
4. Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to the original payment method.

## Workshop

Applies to: Build Your Consumer Evidence File Workshop

### Cancellation by Buyer

- Full refund available up to 48 hours before the scheduled event.
- No refund within 48 hours of the event start time.
- Replay access may be offered at IKE Solutions' discretion.

### Cancellation by IKE Solutions

If IKE Solutions cancels the event, buyers receive a full refund or the option to attend a rescheduled session at no additional cost.

## Free Products

The Consumer Evidence Case Starter Sheet is free. No refund applies.

Refund disputes not resolved by email may be escalated to [owner to specify final dispute mechanism].

## Policy Rationale

The 14-day digital-product window supports low-friction validation purchases without creating significant refund abuse risk. Workshop restrictions protect limited seat capacity and preparation time.
