# DRS-001 — Decision Record Standard

## Purpose

Define the required structure for durable Howard Life Institution decision records so decisions remain auditable, reproducible, and reviewable over time.

## Scope

This standard governs every institutional decision record (`DR-xxxx`) entered into the Decision Register.

## Normative Requirements

1. Every decision record MUST have a unique `Decision ID` in the form `DR-YYYY-NNNN`, where `YYYY` is the four-digit calendar year of record creation and `NNNN` is a zero-padded sequence unique within that year.
2. Every decision record MUST include the mandatory fields in this standard.
3. Every decision record MUST identify who holds decision authority and who authored the record.
4. Every decision record MUST distinguish accepted evidence from rejected evidence.
5. Every decision record MUST preserve reasoning, alternatives, and consequences.
6. Every decision record MUST include status and supersession metadata to maintain history.
7. Decision records MUST be append-only once ratified; post-ratification corrections or reversals MUST be captured by a new decision record that references the prior record.

## Required Structure

Each decision record MUST include, at minimum, the following sections and fields:

| Section | Required Fields |
|---|---|
| Header | Decision ID, Title, Status, Effective Date, Decision Authority, Record Author |
| Decision Context | Question, Trigger/Event, Decision Scope |
| Decision Outcome | Final Decision, Rationale, Consequences |
| Alternatives | Alternatives Considered, Rejection Reason per alternative |
| Evidence | Accepted Evidence, Rejected Evidence, Evidence Quality/Confidence |
| Governance | Constraints/Policies Considered, Risks, Required Safeguards |
| Execution | Implementation Owner, Immediate Actions, Follow-up Actions |
| Lifecycle | Review Date, Supersedes, Superseded By, Draft Change Log |
| Sign-off | Ratifier, Ratification Date, Ratification Decision |

## Status Model

Decision records MUST use one of the following statuses:

- `Draft`
- `Proposed`
- `Ratified`
- `Superseded`
- `Archived`

## Compliance Rule

A decision is not institutionally durable until its record conforms to DRS-001 and reaches `Ratified` status.

## Lifecycle Clarification

- `Change Log` entries are only for edits made before ratification while status is `Draft` or `Proposed`.
- After `Ratified`, the record is immutable; any substantive update requires a new `DR-` entry linked through `Supersedes` and `Superseded By`.
