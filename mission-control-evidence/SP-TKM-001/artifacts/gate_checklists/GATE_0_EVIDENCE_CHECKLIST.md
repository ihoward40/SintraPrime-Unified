## Evidence Handling Rules

- Original screenshots must not be altered.
- Redacted working copies are stored separately.
- If a feature does not appear, record `NOT SHOWN IN PROVIDED EVIDENCE`.
- Do not record `INELIGIBLE` automatically.
- No tax numbers, banking information, or payout-account details shall be preserved.

## Acceptance Status Definitions

- **ACCEPTED** — screenshot is clear, properly redacted, and verifies the required field.
- **ACCEPTED_WITH_LIMITATIONS** — verifies the field but missing one non-critical detail.
- **REDACTION_REQUIRED** — sensitive data is visible; owner must resubmit.
- **INSUFFICIENT** — screenshot does not show the required field.
- **REJECTED** — screenshot is not usable for audit purposes.
