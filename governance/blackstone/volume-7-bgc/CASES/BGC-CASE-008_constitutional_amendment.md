# Case — Constitutional Amendment

## Case ID

BGC-CASE-008

## Title

Constitutional Amendment

## Scenario

A steward proposes adding a new article to BKGC about automated decision review. The proposal would change how AI-generated recommendations are escalated to humans.

## Inputs

- Draft amendment text.
- Existing BKGC Articles XXII and XXIV.
- Impact assessment showing required updates to BGS, BRA, and BCCM.

## Governing Principles and Articles

- BKGC § XXV — Constitutional Amendment
- BKGC-R-023 CDR for Major Decisions
- BGS-S-016 Supersession
- GOVERNANCE_MANIFEST.md Amendment Doctrine

## Evidence Evaluation

| Evidence | Source Category | Weight | Notes |
|----------|-----------------|--------|-------|
| Draft amendment | Governance Proposal | Proposed | Requires ratification |
| Existing articles | Constitutional Text | Controlling | Define current process |
| Impact assessment | Internal Analysis | Informative | Identifies downstream changes |

## Reasoning

1. The amendment is constitutional because it changes BKGC.
2. It requires the constitutional amendment procedure defined in `volume-1-bkgc/RATIFICATION.md`.
3. Downstream contradictions must be resolved before the amendment takes effect.
4. The amendment must be recorded as a CDR.

## Outcome

- Open a formal amendment proposal.
- Require ratification through the constitutional threshold.
- Create a CDR documenting the decision.
- Update BGS, BRA, and BCCM before the amendment becomes effective.
