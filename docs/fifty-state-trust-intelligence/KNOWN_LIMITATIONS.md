# Known Limitations

- New Jersey has not received professional legal review.
- N.J.A.C. 17:33 was located through a regulation mirror and DORES guidance; official administrative-code verification is required for production.
- Specific New Jersey exemption rules for wage garnishment, bank levy, retirement accounts, life insurance, annuities, tenancy by the entirety, homestead/residential exemptions, support orders, and government claims remain research-in-progress.
- Federal bankruptcy, tax, and interstate choice-of-law overlays are not complete.
- Decanting is left as an open New Jersey research question.
- The frontend pilot was not added because backend correctness, data, API, and tests took priority.
- Unsupported private-law claims are quarantined and preserved only to prevent accidental use.
