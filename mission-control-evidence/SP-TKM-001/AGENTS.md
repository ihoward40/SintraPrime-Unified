## Purpose

Governed mission workspace for SP-TKM-001: IKE Solutions TikTok Revenue Engine. Contains strategy, governance standards, scripts, offers, analytics, live agendas, affiliate and sponsorship material, and final mission evidence.

## Ownership

- Mission Owner: Isiah Tarik Howard

- Workstream owners defined in `SP-TKM-001_MASTER_MISSION.md`

## Local Contracts

- All artifacts must conform to `SP-TKM-001_MASTER_MISSION.md`.
- No legal, tax, credit, UCC, debt, or court claim may be published without source verification, jurisdiction/date checks, and Sentinel approval.
- Revenue ownership priority: owned products > workshops > institutional pilots > TikTok Series > affiliate > sponsorship > subscriptions/Gifts > Creator Rewards.
- Every video script must use the script structure defined in the master mission.
- Every link must carry UTM parameters: `utm_source=tiktok`, `utm_medium=organic`, `utm_campaign=consumer_evidence`, `utm_content=<video_id>`.
- Every gate (G-0 through G-9) requires an artifact and verification evidence, not just generated text.

## Work Guidance

- Workstream Zero and One must pass before any public content is published.
- Content production must begin only after TASK-001 through TASK-015 are completed and Day One status report is issued.
- Daily operating loop and weekly operating review must run on schedule.
- Stop conditions are binding; publication halts immediately when any trigger is met.
- Human review is mandatory for HIGH-risk content, legal interpretations, sponsor contracts, tax content, court-content scripts, UCC filing instructions, debt-payment recommendations, and testimonials.

## Verification

- Gate checklists are stored in `artifacts/gate_checklists/` as markdown files named `GATE_<number>_CHECKLIST.md`.
- Screenshots go into `artifacts/screenshots/`, URL lists into `artifacts/urls/`, and verification records into `artifacts/verification/`.
- Dashboard schema and weekly reports go into `reports/tiktok/`.
- Final acceptance requires all checkboxes in Section 21 of the master mission to be ticked with evidence and human owner signature.
