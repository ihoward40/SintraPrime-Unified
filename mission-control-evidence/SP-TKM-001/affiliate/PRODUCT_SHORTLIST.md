## 1. Scoring Model

Each product is scored 0–5 across ten criteria. Minimum approval score: **35/50**.

| Criterion | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Audience fit | 1 | Relevant to document/evidence workflow |
| Demonstration value | 1 | Easy to show in a short video |
| Quality | 1 | Brand/seller reputation |
| Price reasonableness | 1 | Affordable for target audience |
| Commission | 1 | Reasonable commission rate |
| Seller reliability | 1 | Established seller with good fulfillment |
| Shipping performance | 1 | Reliable delivery |
| Return risk | 1 | Low complaint/return rate |
| Policy risk | 1 | No deceptive or legally questionable marketing |
| Brand credibility | 1 | Aligns with IKE Solutions educational positioning |
