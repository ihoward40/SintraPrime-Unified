## 4. Educational Disclaimer

> This LIVE provides general consumer education, not individualized legal, tax, or financial advice. Outcomes depend on your facts, jurisdiction, and evidence.

## 5. Redacted Document Demonstration Rules

- Use only synthetic or fully redacted sample documents.
- No real account numbers, SSNs, signatures, or addresses visible.
- Do not display a viewer’s actual documents on screen.
- Explain what the document type is and why the date, party name, or reference number matters.

## 6. Question-Routing Framework

| Category | Handling |
|---|---|
| A — General education | Answer live with a concise explanation. |
| B — Document-specific but low risk | Answer with limits; remind viewer to verify in their jurisdiction. |
| C — Legal-strategy question | Explain general rules and refer for individualized review. |
| D — Emergency or deadline matter | Advise prompt qualified assistance; do not delay. |
| E — Prohibited or fraudulent request | Refuse and redirect; mute if necessary after warning. |

## 7. Moderation Rules

- No personal document sharing on screen.
- No advice to ignore a court date or stop making payments.
- No naming specific individuals or companies as fraudulent without verified evidence.
- No testimonials presented as typical results.
- No payment requests in exchange for guaranteed outcomes.
- Pin the educational disclaimer comment.
