# TikTok Human Review Trigger List

Mission: SP-TKM-001
Owner: Sentinel
Effective Date: 2026-07-27

## Triggers Requiring Mandatory Human Review

The following content may not be published or approved by agents alone. A human reviewer must sign off before production or publication.

| # | Trigger | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal interpretation of statute, regulation, or court rule | Risk of misstatement or jurisdiction error |
| 2 | Individual case analysis or advice | Could constitute unauthorized practice |
| 3 | Sponsor contract or brand collaboration | Commercial and legal obligations |
| 4 | TikTok Series pricing or refund terms | Platform and consumer-protection risk |
| 5 | Tax form instructions or tax content | Specialized, high-risk subject |
| 6 | Court-content scripts (deadlines, filings, jurisdiction) | Could jeopardize rights |
| 7 | UCC filing instructions against a named party | High legal and fraud risk |
| 8 | Debt-payment recommendations (stop/reduce payments) | Could harm consumer's position |
| 9 | Claims about a specific company or person | Defamation and evidence risk |
| 10 | Testimonials or case results | Advertising substantiation risk |
| 11 | Content involving children or minors | Privacy and safety risk |
| 12 | Content based on private customer records | Privacy breach risk |
| 13 | HIGH-risk classification per the claim matrix | Inherent elevated risk |
| 14 | V4 disputed or unresolved legal theory | Lack of settled authority |
| 15 | Borderline MODERATE/HIGH content where Sentinel is uncertain | Better to escalate |

## Human Review Process

1. Justice Scribe or Revenue Architect flags the trigger in the script or offer.
2. Athena compiles or confirms the source packet.
3. Sentinel assigns the risk rating and adds the trigger label.
4. Hermes routes the item to the human reviewer with full context.
5. Human reviewer records one outcome:
   - **APPROVE**
   - **APPROVE WITH EDITS** (edits listed)
   - **REJECT**
6. Edits are applied and re-checked.
7. Item proceeds only after written human approval is recorded.

## Recording Human Review

Human review decisions are recorded in the source packet or artifact with:

- Reviewer name
- Date
- Outcome
- Edits required
- Signature or written confirmation

## Stop Condition

If a human reviewer is unavailable and the content cannot wait, the item is rejected or postponed. Agents may not substitute their judgment for required human review.
