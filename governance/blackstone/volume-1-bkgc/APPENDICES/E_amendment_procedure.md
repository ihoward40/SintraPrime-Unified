# Appendix E — Constitutional Amendment Procedure (Normative)

## Derived From

BKGC Article XXV and `RATIFICATION.md`.

## Amendment Classes and Thresholds

| Class | Change Type | Approval |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Editorial | Typographical or formatting | Maintainer |
| Minor | Clarification, examples, non-normative guidance | One reviewer |
| Major | New Article or material change | Two reviewers + governance steward |
| Constitutional | Core principle change | Ratification vote |

## Amendment Process

1. Propose CDR describing the conflict or need.
2. Draft amendment text.
3. Assess impact on all downstream volumes.
4. Review and approve per class threshold.
5. Update `CHANGELOG.md`.
6. Sign `RATIFICATION.md` if required.
7. Notify implementations via BCCM update cycle.

## Compatibility Rule

A constitutional amendment MUST NOT take effect until downstream contradictions are resolved or explicitly deferred by CDR.
