# Registry — Source Taxonomy

## Authority

This registry is authoritative for source category codes and default weights used across the Blackstone ecosystem.

## Derived From

BKGC Article V, Article VIII; BKGC Appendix B; BGS 4.

## Taxonomy

| Code | Category | Description | Default Weight |
|------|----------|-------------|----------------|
| GOV | Government Publication | Statutes, regulations, agency guidance, official records | Controlling within jurisdiction |
| JUD | Judicial Decision | Court opinions, orders, published reasoning | Controlling or persuasive by level |
| ACA | Academic Scholarship | Peer-reviewed or scholarly analysis | Persuasive or scholarly |
| HAR | Historical Archive | Original historical records and documents | Historical |
| COM | Commercial Publication | Professionally published treatises, manuals, guides | Persuasive or educational |
| PRI | Private Institutional Document | Internal manuals, policies, unpublished institutional materials | Requires provenance review |
| IND | Independent Research | Publications by independent researchers | Emerging or scholarly |
| AIG | AI-Generated Output | Summaries, analyses, reasoning produced by AI | Derivative; requires verification |
| CMT | Community Discussion | Forums, commentary, informal exchange | Educational or unverified |

## Rule

No category is accepted or rejected solely because of its origin. Default weights are rebuttable by provenance, corroboration, and jurisdictional relevance.

## Change Control

Additions or changes to this taxonomy require a CDR and update to BGS 4 and BKGC Appendix B if applicable.
