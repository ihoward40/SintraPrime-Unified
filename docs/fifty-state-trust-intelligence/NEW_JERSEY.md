# New Jersey Pilot

New Jersey is the Phase 1 pilot jurisdiction. Status: `TESTED`, pending command validation and commit evidence. It is not human reviewed and not production eligible.

Covered domains:

- Trust formation and administration under the New Jersey Uniform Trust Code.
- Trust creditor exposure, including spendthrift, discretionary, mandatory, overdue distributions, settlor-creditor, revocable exposure, and settlor-benefit warnings.
- New Jersey UVTA warnings for actual-intent and constructive transfer analysis.
- UCC Article 9 filing office, debtor naming, trust debtors, financing statements, amendments, continuations, terminations, search/rejection procedures, electronic filing, and fees.

Primary authority records are in `data/jurisdictions/new_jersey/authorities.json`. Rules are in `data/jurisdictions/new_jersey/rules.json`. Research coverage is in `data/jurisdictions/new_jersey/research_manifest.json`.

All New Jersey legal output displays: Educational and issue-spotting output only. Not a legal opinion.
