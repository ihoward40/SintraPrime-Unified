# Governance And Approvals

The Principal is the human authority. The Principal role may be represented in a plan as an approval gate, but no model can replace or impersonate that authority.

Approval is required for high-risk actions, restricted or privileged sensitivity, unresolved disagreement, and any result that would otherwise cause external impact. Approval records must preserve requested action, reason, risk level, status, Principal identity when decided, decision reason, and event evidence.

Milestone One may recommend, simulate, and request approval only. Deployment, merge, live provider use, paid API use, and external action remain blocked.
