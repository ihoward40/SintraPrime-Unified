## 3. Sponsor Exclusion List

Reject sponsors that offer or promote:

- Debt-elimination schemes
- False tradeline services
- CPN sellers or fake credit-profile vendors
- Unlicensed legal-service schemes
- Guaranteed funding services
- False grant services
- Pyramid or recruiting-based offers
- Products requiring deceptive claims
- Any sponsor requiring suppression of material limitations

## 4. Sponsorship Packages

### Package A — Educational Integration

- One 60–90 second video
- One link placement in bio or pinned comment
- One pinned comment with disclosure
- Performance report (views, engagement, link clicks)

### Package B — Workflow Demonstration

- One full demonstration video (1–3 minutes)
- Two short follow-up clips
- One LIVE mention
- Performance report

### Package C — Educational Campaign

- Four-video series
- One LIVE segment (5–10 minutes)
- Landing-page placement
- Email inclusion to waitlist
- 30-day performance report

No perpetual usage rights are transferred for a basic one-post fee.

## 6. Pre-Acceptance Sentinel Review Checklist

Before accepting any sponsor, Sentinel must verify:

- [ ] Sponsor identity and registered business
- [ ] Product legitimacy and complaint history
- [ ] Required claims and whether they are V1–V5 compliant
- [ ] Disclosure requirements under FTC and TikTok Branded Content Policy
- [ ] Usage rights scope and duration
- [ ] Payment terms and invoicing
- [ ] Cancellation and refund terms
- [ ] Exclusivity or non-compete scope
- [ ] Indemnity and content ownership
- [ ] Whether the sponsor category is on the exclusion list
