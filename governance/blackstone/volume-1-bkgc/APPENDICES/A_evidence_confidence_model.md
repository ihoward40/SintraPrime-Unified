# Appendix A — Evidence Confidence Model (Informative)

## Derived From

BKGC Article VII § 7.1 and Article XV § 15.2.

## Confidence Scores

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| A | Multiple primary sources corroborated |
| B | Primary plus persuasive support |
| C | Persuasive authority only |
| D | Educational material only |
| E | Community or scholarly discussion |
| F | Unverified claim |

## Application

Confidence scores SHALL be derived from evidence quality, not intuition. A confidence score is distinct from a claim status. A claim may be historically documented with low corroboration (Score C or D) or emerging with high methodological support (Score B).

## Downstream Use

BGS 3 defines the operational scoring rules. BCCM certifies that agents apply the model consistently.
