# Workshop Facilitator Guide

Mission: SP-TKM-001
Owner: Justice Scribe
Status: Internal draft

## Overview

- **Workshop:** Build Your Consumer Evidence File
- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Format:** Live online
- **Audience:** Consumers organizing records for disputes, collections, credit issues, or complaints

## Pre-Session Checklist

- [ ] Zoom or streaming platform tested
- [ ] Screen sharing enabled for redacted document demonstrations
- [ ] Participant workbook sent 24 hours before session
- [ ] Calendar invite sent
- [ ] Recording enabled with participant consent noted
- [ ] Pinned educational disclaimer comment prepared
- [ ] Moderation plan active

## Timing Cues

| Time | Segment | Facilitator Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–5:00 | Welcome and disclaimer | Read disclaimer; set ground rules. |
| 5:00–15:00 | Define the issue | Teach one-sentence summary; participants draft. |
| 15:00–25:00 | Identify parties | Walk through party list; participants fill in. |
| 25:00–40:00 | Build timeline | Demonstrate timeline method; participants add events. |
| 40:00–55:00 | Inventory documents | Review five-document starter set; participants check inventory. |
| 55:00–65:00 | Redact safely | Show redaction demonstration; participants identify redactions. |
| 65:00–80:00 | Claims vs. evidence | Demonstrate claim/evidence table; participants complete one row. |
| 80:00–90:00 | Missing evidence, next steps, Q&A | List next steps; answer general questions; mention next offers. |

## Redacted Document Demonstrations

Use only synthetic or fully redacted sample documents. Demonstrate:

1. A billing statement with account number redacted.
2. A collection letter with address and signature redacted.
3. A certified mail receipt with addresses redacted.
4. A credit report entry with account number redacted.

## Question-Routing Framework

| Category | Handling |
|---|---|
| A — General education | Answer live with a concise explanation. |
| B — Document-specific but low risk | Answer with limits; remind viewer to verify in their jurisdiction. |
| C — Legal-strategy question | Explain general rules and refer for individualized review. |
| D — Emergency or deadline matter | Advise prompt qualified assistance; do not delay. |
| E — Prohibited or fraudulent request | Refuse and redirect; mute if necessary after warning. |

## Moderation Rules

- No personal document sharing on screen.
- No advice to ignore a court date or stop making payments.
- No naming specific individuals or companies as fraudulent without verified evidence.
- No testimonials presented as typical results.
- No payment requests in exchange for guaranteed outcomes.
- Pin the educational disclaimer comment.
