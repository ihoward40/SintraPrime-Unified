# Known Limitations

- New Jersey has not received real licensed-attorney legal review.
- New Jersey remains `TESTED`, not `HUMAN_REVIEWED` and not `PRODUCTION_ELIGIBLE`.
- N.J.A.C. 17:33 was constrained by official-source access. Official OAL/Treasury sources identify the official Administrative Code access path and adoption notice, but Phase 2A did not capture full current official text.
- Several creditor and exemption authorities remain locator-copy or source-limited records pending official compiled-source review.
- Wage execution, bank levy, support, government claim, tax claim, pension, property exemption, insurance, annuity, tenancy-by-entirety, homestead, IRA, and bankruptcy intersections are issue spotting only.
- Federal bankruptcy rules are labeled `FEDERAL_BANKRUPTCY_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; no bankruptcy filing advice is encoded.
- New Jersey decanting is not encoded as available. Phase 2A did not locate clear current New Jersey statutory decanting authority.
- Digital asset and special-needs trust coverage remains partial and human-review gated.
- The frontend dependency tree was incomplete in this environment; `npm run build` could not complete because local `tsc` was unavailable and dependency repair hit Windows/npm cleanup problems.
- Unsupported private-law claims are quarantined and cannot be approved without source reclassification.
