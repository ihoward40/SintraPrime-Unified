# Professional Review

Phase 1 contains no authorized human legal-review completion mechanism and no completed professional review records.

Review gates:

- Any rule requiring legal interpretation remains `requires_human_review: true`.
- `PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFIED` does not permit production use.
- `HUMAN_REVIEWED` and `PRODUCTION_ELIGIBLE` require a reviewer identity, reviewer role, findings, conditions, timestamp, and repository governance approval.
- Conflicts, missing dates, official-code limitations, bankruptcy overlays, and unsupported private-law claims cannot be bypassed by automated confidence.

New Jersey must remain below `HUMAN_REVIEWED` until a qualified reviewer completes review under an authorized workflow.
