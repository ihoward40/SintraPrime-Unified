# DR-0001 — Approve BOE-001 as the Master Implementation Program

**Decision ID:** DR-0001  
**Status:** APPROVED  
**Effective Date:** 2026-09-27  
**Approved By:** Isiah Howard  
**Program Artifact:** `BOE/00-Program/BOE-001_Master_Implementation_Spec.md`

## Question

Should BOE-001 be approved as the master implementation program for the Howard Life Institution?

## Decision

**Yes.** BOE-001 is approved as the master implementation program for the Howard Life Institution and becomes the governing implementation baseline for Sprint 1 BOE work.

## Basis

- Parent issue `#193` defines BOE-001 as the executive foundation required to establish repeatable, evidence-based decision cycles.
- The BOE standing engineering rule requires each BOE artifact to enable, support, execute, or preserve a decision.
- BOE-001 defines the initial build order, deferred work boundaries, and Milestone M1 acceptance criteria needed to manage BOE delivery as a coherent program.

## Consequences

1. Sprint 1 BOE execution must trace back to `BOE/00-Program/BOE-001_Master_Implementation_Spec.md`.
2. Changes to BOE scope, sequence, or M1 acceptance criteria must update BOE-001 and record a new or superseding decision record.
3. Work on the Executive Dashboard, Confidence Ledger, Operational History, and Board Intelligence remains deferred until BOE-001 Sprint 1 artifacts are operational.

## Alternatives Considered

1. **Operate without a master program.** Rejected because it would scatter scope, ordering, and acceptance criteria across issue text and ad hoc updates.
2. **Approve downstream artifacts individually without a controlling program document.** Rejected because BOE-001 is needed to establish the authoritative baseline those artifacts must follow.
