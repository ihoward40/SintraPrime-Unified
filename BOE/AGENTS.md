# BOE — DOX Contract

## Purpose

Define the Executive Foundation artifacts for the Howard Life Institution, including standards and records that enable, support, execute, or preserve decisions.

## Ownership

- Repository owner (Isiah Howard) holds final ratification authority for institutional standards.
- Contributors may draft and revise BOE artifacts under repository governance.

## Local Contracts

- BOE artifacts MUST align with issue #193 standing rule: each artifact enables, supports, executes, or preserves a decision.
- Decision Register standards and records live under `01-Decision-Register/`.
- Institutional standards use stable identifiers (for example `DRS-001`).

## Work Guidance

- Keep standards implementation-neutral and durable.
- Use RFC 2119 keywords (`MUST`, `SHOULD`, `MAY`) for normative requirements.
- Include explicit required fields and lifecycle expectations for any durable record format.
