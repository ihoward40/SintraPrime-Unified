# Build Your Consumer Evidence File — Workshop Curriculum

Mission: SP-TKM-001
Owner: Justice Scribe + Revenue Architect
Status: Internal draft — no public scheduling in Phase Two

## Workshop Identity

- **Title:** Build Your Consumer Evidence File
- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Format:** Live online session with replay access
- **Validation Price:** $79
- **Target Audience:** Consumers who want a guided, organized start to a case file for disputes, collections, credit issues, or complaints

## Learning Objectives

By the end of the workshop, participants will be able to:

1. Define the issue they are trying to solve in one sentence.
2. List every party involved in their matter.
3. Build a chronological timeline with supporting documents.
4. Inventory the documents they have and identify missing records.
5. Redact sensitive information safely.
6. Compare a claim against the available evidence.
7. Identify the next step appropriate for their situation.
8. Prepare organized materials for a qualified professional review.

## Session Outline

### 1. Welcome and Disclaimer (5 minutes)

- Greet participants.
- State the workshop's educational purpose.
- Read the standard educational disclaimer.
- Explain ground rules: no individual legal advice, no document sharing on screen, no recording by participants without consent.

### 2. Define the Issue (10 minutes)

- Teach the one-sentence case summary.
- Identify the desired outcome.
- Distinguish the problem from the evidence.
- Exercise: participants draft their own case summary.

### 3. Identify Every Party (10 minutes)

- Original creditor or company.
- Collector or servicer.
- Credit bureaus.
- Regulators or agencies.
- Attorneys or advocates.
- Exercise: participants fill in the party-list template.

### 4. Build the Timeline (15 minutes)

- Why chronology matters.
- How to place dates, events, and documents.
- How to spot gaps and contradictions.
- Exercise: participants add five key events to their timeline.

### 5. Inventory the Documents (15 minutes)

- The five-document starter set.
- Expanding the inventory.
- Labeling and storage.
- Exercise: participants check which documents they have.

### 6. Redact Safely (10 minutes)

- What to redact and why.
- Tools and methods.
- Common mistakes.
- Exercise: participants identify what should be redacted on a sample document.

### 7. Compare Claims Against Evidence (15 minutes)

- Separating claims from proof.
- Identifying unsupported claims.
- Flagging missing evidence.
- Exercise: participants match one claim to available evidence.

### 8. Identify Missing Documents and Next Steps (10 minutes)

- How to request missing records.
- Public complaint options.
- When to seek professional help.
- Exercise: participants list two next steps and one missing document.

### 9. Q&A and Next Offers (10 minutes)

- Answer general education questions.
- Mention free Starter Sheet.
- Mention Intake Pack and Starter Kit for deeper templates.
- Mention next Consumer Evidence Clinic.

## Recording and Replay Policy

- Workshops are recorded for registered participants.
- Replay access is granted for 30 days after the live event unless otherwise stated.
- Participants may not record or redistribute the session without written permission.
- Redacted document demonstrations may be reused in future educational content.

## Terms and Disclaimer

All workshop materials include the standard educational disclaimer. The workshop does not provide individualized legal, tax, or financial advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.
