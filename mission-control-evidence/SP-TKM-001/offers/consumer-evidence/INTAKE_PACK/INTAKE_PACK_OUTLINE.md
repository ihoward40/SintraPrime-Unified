# Consumer Evidence Intake Pack — Product Outline

Mission: SP-TKM-001
Owner: Revenue Architect
Status: Draft / Validation Price Only

## 1. Product Identity

- **Name:** Consumer Evidence Intake Pack
- **Validation Price:** $9
- **Format:** Digital download (PDF + editable worksheet files)
- **Target Buyer:** A consumer who wants a guided, organized way to start a case file for a dispute, collection matter, credit report issue, or complaint.

## 2. What Is Included

1. Consumer Evidence Case Starter Sheet (expanded PDF)
2. Party-list template
3. Timeline template
4. Document inventory checklist
5. Communication log template
6. Deadlines tracker
7. Redaction guide
8. Quick-start guide: "How to use this pack in 30 minutes"
9. Glossary of common terms (charge-off, validation, assignment, judgment, etc.)
10. Directory of public complaint and record-request resources

## 3. What Is NOT Included

- Individualized legal, tax, or financial advice
- Attorney-client relationship or representation
- Guarantee of any outcome, dispute result, or record removal
- Draft letters tailored to a specific case
- Court filing preparation
- Debt negotiation services
