# Glossary — Core Terms

## Authority (Normative)

**BKR-TERM-002** This glossary is the master reference for terms used across the Blackstone Governance Library.

## A

**BKR-TERM-003 Agent.** An AI or human actor that participates in the governance process.

**Amendment.** A formal change to a governance document, classified as editorial, minor, major, or constitutional.

**Article.** A major division of the Constitution.

**Assumption.** A premise accepted without direct evidence, explicitly declared.

## C

**CDR (Constitutional Decision Record).** A permanent record of a major governance decision.

**BKR-TERM-004 Claim.** An assertion that can be supported, challenged, revised, or superseded by evidence.

**Confidence.** An evidence-derived measure of strength, not intuition.

**Constitutional Epistemology.** The philosophy that knowledge becomes trustworthy through governed process rather than popularity.

**Controlling Authority.** A source that governs a question within its jurisdiction.

**BKR-TERM-011 Counter-Evidence.** Evidence that challenges a claim.

## D

**BKR-TERM-012 Decision.** A commitment to a conclusion or recommendation.

## E

**BKR-TERM-005 Evidence.** Material that supports or challenges a claim, with identifiable provenance.

## F

**Failure Mode.** A known way in which governance can break down.

## G

**Governed Knowledge.** Conclusions produced, evaluated, preserved, and reviewed according to the constitutional process.

## J

**BKR-TERM-008 Jurisdiction.** The legally or institutionally relevant scope within which an authority governs a question.

## K

**BKR-TERM-009 Knowledge Core (BKC).** The shared semantic model defining concepts, relationships, and lifecycles.

**Knowledge Object.** Any entity that carries meaning and can be governed.

## O

**Objection.** A challenge to a claim, decision, or interpretation.

**Ontology.** A formal description of concepts and relationships in a domain.

## P

**Persuasive Authority.** A respected but non-controlling source.

**Private Research Doctrine.** The principle that private and minority sources are legitimate research inputs evaluated by evidence quality, not origin.

**BKR-TERM-007 Provenance.** The documented origin, custody, and history of evidence.

## R

**Reasoning Engine.** The engine that produces inspectable reasoning chains.

**Reference Implementation.** SintraPrime-Unified, designated as the executable implementation of the architecture.

**Registry.** An authoritative list of definitions, identifiers, or classifications.

**Review.** Examination of a knowledge object for conformance.

**Revision.** An updated version of a knowledge object.

## S

**Source.** Origin of evidence.

**Supersession.** Replacement of a knowledge object by a newer one.

## T

**Taxonomy.** A controlled classification scheme.

## V

**Verification.** Independent confirmation that a conclusion follows from evidence and process.
