# Federal Research Standard

Prefer official U.S. Code, federal judiciary rules, agency regulations, official forms, and official guidance. The U.S. Code and judiciary sources are recorded as primary legal authority where captured. eCFR records are `PRIMARY_SOURCE_LOCATED` and `LOCATOR_ONLY` because eCFR electronic text is authoritative but unofficial.

Each topic records primary authorities, source limitations, open questions, and a human-review requirement. Federal benefit, bankruptcy, tax, and SCRA topics are issue spotting only. No source-verification state represents attorney, CPA, bankruptcy, or tax approval.
