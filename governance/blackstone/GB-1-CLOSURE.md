# GB-1 — Project Closure & Baseline Establishment Record

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**Classification:** Meta-governance (not part of the frozen GB-1 baseline)
**Supersedes:** None. Records the transition from governance construction to operational validation.

## 1. Project Phases

The Blackstone Governance Library effort proceeded through four distinct phases:

| Phase | Question | Result |
|-------|----------|--------|
| I — Vision | What should good governance look like? | BKGC concept. |
| II — Architecture | How should governance be organized? | Seven-volume library; layer separation; constitutional hierarchy. |
| III — Governance | How does governance govern itself? | Amendment process; candidate registry; review triggers; integrity mechanism; change control. |
| IV — Operations | Can governance improve real work? | Current phase. Success measured by outcomes, not documents. |

## 2. Milestone Statement

> Blackstone Governance Baseline 1 (GB-1) established the project's first operational governance baseline by separating constitutional governance, operational standards, semantic definitions, implementation guidance, certification, registries, and case studies into a coherent, versioned architecture. The governance mechanism demonstrated self-application through correction of a post-ratification amendment attempt, and the project formally transitioned from governance construction to operational validation. Future governance evolution is contingent upon recurring operational evidence rather than anticipated needs or isolated cases.

## 3. Primary Metric — Decision Rework Rate

The single indicator to watch over the next year:

> **Decision Rework Rate** — how often a decision is revisited because important evidence was missing at the time it was made.

Expected signal of success: fewer premature decisions, fewer contradictory positions, fewer missing-evidence requests, and less rework caused by avoidable uncertainty. This metric is the operational validation input for the `RETROSPECTIVES/` process and the RS-1 elevation decision.

## 4. Workstream Closure Directive

> **Governance Workstream: CLOSED.**
