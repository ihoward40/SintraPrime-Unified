# Tax Operations Control Template

This is a generic operations template only. Do not store taxpayer names, identifiers, notices, transcripts, bank records, payment confirmations or other confidential tax data in this public repository.

## Gate Zero — taxpayer/notice verification
Verify from the source notice: notice type/date, response date, taxpayer type, form, tax year, amount and identifier (redacted in operational reports). A taxpayer/form mismatch blocks response strategy.

## Evidence phase
Collect privately: account transcript, record of account, return transcript, wage/income transcript if relevant, filed return, payment confirmations, bank proof, vouchers and prior correspondence.

## Payment reconciliation
For each claimed payment record privately: date, amount, intended period, method, confirmation, bank debit, transcript match, evidence reference and status (PROVEN/PARTIAL/MISSING/CONTRADICTED).

## Governance
Claimed payments remain CLAIMED, NOT PROVEN until matched to objective evidence. Do not call, submit, promise payment or state a final tax/legal conclusion until the applicable approval gate is satisfied. Avoid unsupported jurisdictional/UCC/A4V/redemption/strawman theories; use factual transcript and payment evidence.

## Collection posture
Privately verify current balance, notices, lien/levy status and collection assignment before choosing an action.
