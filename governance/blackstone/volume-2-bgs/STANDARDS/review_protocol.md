# Standard 2 — Review Protocol

## Purpose

Define the operational review workflow for governed conclusions.

## Derived From

BGS 6, BKGC Articles XIII, XVIII, and XXII.

## Review Levels

| Level | Trigger | Actor | Output |
|-------|---------|-------|--------|
| R0 | Any registered evidence | Automated pipeline | Conformance report |
| R1 | Claim formation | Certified agent | Review record |
| R2 | Consequential / novel / high-risk | Qualified human | Approval or remediation request |
| R3 | Constitutional / major governance | Governance panel | Binding decision recorded as CDR |

## R2 Requirements

1. Reviewer identity MUST be recorded.
2. Reviewer MUST inspect evidence, counter-evidence, and reasoning chain.
3. Reviewer MUST confirm or downgrade claim status.
4. Reviewer MUST sign review record.

## Escalation

A lower-level reviewer MUST escalate to the next level when:

- Evidence is insufficient for the claim type.
- Counter-evidence is material.
- Jurisdiction is uncertain.
- A constitutional question is implicated.
