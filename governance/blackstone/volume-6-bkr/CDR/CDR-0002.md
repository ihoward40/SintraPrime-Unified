# CDR-0002 — Separate Constitution from Standards and Implementation

## Problem

A single document cannot remain stable enough to serve as a constitution while also containing the detailed operational rules needed by engineers.

## Alternatives Considered

1. **One living document.** Rejected because frequent edits would erode constitutional authority.
2. **Constitution plus separate standards, core, architecture, and compliance volumes.** Selected because it preserves stability at the top while allowing continuous evolution below.

## Decision

BKGC changes by constitutional amendment only (major versions). BGS, BKC, BRA, BCCM, BKR, and BGC evolve under their own cadences and trace back to BKGC.

## Governing Principles

- BKGC § 25.1 Amendment Classes
- BKGC Version Policy (BKGC major versions only)

## Expected Consequences

- The Constitution becomes a durable reference.
- Engineering agility is preserved in downstream volumes.
- Amendment thresholds match the scope and consequence of each change.

## Revisit Conditions

If a downstream volume repeatedly requires constitutional-level changes, reconsider whether its content belongs in BKGC.
