## Amendment Procedure

### Editorial Amendment

- Corrects typographical, formatting, or cross-reference errors.
- MAY be approved by the document maintainer.
- Requires changelog entry only.

### Minor Amendment

- Clarifies existing language without altering substance.
- Adds examples, diagrams, or non-normative guidance.
- Requires review by at least one qualified reviewer.
- Requires CDR.

### Major Amendment

- Adds a new Article or materially changes an existing Article.
- Changes the relationship between the Constitution and downstream volumes.
- Requires review by at least two qualified reviewers.
- Requires approval by the governance steward.
- Requires CDR and backward-compatibility assessment.

### Constitutional Amendment

- Alters a core principle in `GOVERNANCE_PRINCIPLES.md` or a foundational Article.
- Requires formal ratification vote.
- Requires CDR, impact analysis on all downstream volumes, and version increment.

## Ratification Threshold

BKGC v2.0.0 SHALL be considered ratified when:

1. The full seven-volume library structure is established.
2. `GOVERNANCE_MANIFEST.md` is accepted.
3. A qualified human reviewer approves the constitutional text.
4. The ratification record is signed.
