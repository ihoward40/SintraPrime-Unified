# Blackstone Governance Library — Charter

## Purpose of This Document

This Charter describes why the Blackstone Governance Library exists. It is descriptive, not normative. For binding rules, see `volume-1-bkgc/BKGC.md`.

## Why the Library Exists

SintraPrime-Unified is designed to make consequential decisions using evidence, reasoning, and accountable review. As the system grows in jurisdiction, responsibility, and automation, it needs a durable way to:

- keep terminology consistent across agents, models, APIs, and humans;
- distinguish controlled process from desired conclusion;
- preserve provenance so conclusions can be audited;
- handle conflicts between sources, statutes, and interpretations;
- certify that agents and implementations meet the same standards;
- record major governance decisions so institutional memory survives beyond any single contributor or session.

The Blackstone Governance Library is the shared reference that makes these capabilities explicit, testable, and improvable over time.

## What Problems It Solves

1. **Terminology drift.** Without a common semantic core, one agent may treat "verified" as a heuristic while another treats it as a formal gate. BKC resolves that.
2. **Authority confusion.** Legal, scholarly, educational, and historical sources have different weights in different jurisdictions. The library makes the hierarchy explicit.
3. **Undocumented decisions.** Major choices about evidence handling, review thresholds, and agent boundaries are captured as Constitutional Decision Records rather than scattered in chat history.
4. **Process inconsistency.** Evidence intake, scoring, review, and failure remediation follow standards derived from the Constitution.
5. **Audit fragility.** Every governed conclusion must be traceable to evidence, reasoning, reviewer identity, and constitutional provisions.

## Scope

The library governs knowledge claims and the processes used to evaluate them within the Blackstone ecosystem. It applies to:

- AI agents and human contributors;
- internal tools, APIs, and registries;
- reference implementations and certified alternatives;
- legal research, financial analysis, operational recommendations, and historical investigation.

It does not govern:

- matters outside the ecosystem's jurisdiction unless explicitly extended;
- personal opinions or undocumented intuitions;
- decisions made outside the constitutional process.

## Intended Users

- **Governance stewards** maintain the library and ratify amendments.
- **Engineers** implement engines, APIs, and registries that conform to the architecture.
- **Agents** reason over claims according to the rules.
- **Reviewers** inspect conclusions and challenge deviations.
- **Auditors** verify compliance and traceability.

## What Constitutional Compliance Means

A process, implementation, or conclusion is constitutionally compliant when it:

- uses only vocabulary defined or registered in BKC or BKR;
- handles evidence according to BGS;
- applies authority rules from BKGC;
- preserves provenance and review records;
- resolves conflicts through the upstream-first rule;
- can be traced back to a constitutional requirement identifier.

Compliance is about process adherence, not viewpoint agreement.

## How Future Contributors Should Approach Amendments

Treat the Constitution as a constitution: amend rarely, deliberately, and with broad review. Treat standards, the knowledge core, architecture, compliance, registry, and casebook as living documents that evolve within the constitutional frame. Every amendment should answer:

- What problem is being solved?
- Which existing provision does it derive from or modify?
- What alternatives were considered?
- Why was this option selected?
- What are the expected consequences?
- Under what conditions should this decision be revisited?

When in doubt, write a Constitutional Decision Record.

## Relationship to the Constitution

The Charter is mission. The Constitution is law. The Standards are procedure. The Knowledge Core is vocabulary. The Architecture is executable structure. The Compliance Manual is verification. The Registry is canonical record. The Casebook is illustration.

No part of this Charter overrides any provision of BKGC.
