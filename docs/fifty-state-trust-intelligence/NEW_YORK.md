# New York Phase 2B Jurisdiction Notes

Status: `TESTED`, not human reviewed, not production eligible.

## Domains Encoded

- Trust creation and validity: EPTL 7-1.17 lifetime trust execution, amendments, revocation, capacity/purpose issue spotting, and explicit note that New York is not treated as a Uniform Trust Code state.
- Trustee administration: EPTL Article 7 and SCPA fiduciary administration issue spotting for accounting, removal, beneficiary information, compensation, bonds, and related proceedings.
- Creditor protection: EPTL 7-1.5 spendthrift/statutory restraint, EPTL 7-3.1 self-settled exposure, CPLR bank restraints, income execution, exemptions, tenancy-by-entirety issue spotting, retirement/insurance/annuity intersections, and UVTA transfer review.
- Trust taxation: official IT-205-I fiduciary instructions issue-spot resident/nonresident trust status, New York source income, fiduciary filing, and NYC/Yonkers implications.
- UCC Article 9: filing office, local filings, fixture and transmitting-utility caution, debtor naming, trust and estate debtor names, continuation windows, refusal/acceptance, forms, fees, e-filing, search, and privacy cautions.
- Existing filing assessment: NY rules support the UCC assessment workflow that distinguishes filing-office acceptance from attachment, security-agreement evidence, value, debtor rights, perfection, priority, and privacy risk.
