# TikTok Advertising Disclosure Standard

Mission: SP-TKM-001
Owner: Sentinel
Effective Date: 2026-07-27

## 1. When Disclosure Is Required

Disclosure is required for any content that:

- Promotes an affiliate product or service.
- Is sponsored or paid for by a third party.
- Includes a gifted product or service.
- Is part of a brand collaboration or TikTok One project.
- Contains a paid partnership or product placement.

## 2. Platform Disclosure Tools

- Use TikTok's "Paid partnership" or "Branded content" toggle when available.
- Do not rely solely on hashtags or comments if the platform provides a built-in disclosure tool.

## 3. Verbal and On-Screen Disclosure

In addition to platform tools, include a clear statement in the video:

- Affiliate: "I may earn a commission if you purchase through this link."
- Sponsored: "This video is sponsored by [brand]."
- Gifted: "I received this product at no cost."
- Brand collaboration: "Paid partnership with [brand]."

Place the disclosure near the beginning of the video or in a clearly visible on-screen text overlay.

## 4. Link and Comment Disclosures

- Pinned comments must repeat the disclosure.
- Link-in-bio pages for affiliate or sponsored offers must include a clear disclosure near the CTA.
- Do not bury disclosure in a long caption or behind a "more" button.

## 5. What Must Not Be Hidden

- Payment or free product arrangement
- Affiliate commission structure
- Sponsor review or approval rights
- Exclusivity or category restrictions
- Material limitations of the promoted product

## 6. Examples

### Affiliate video

> Thanks to [scanner brand] for making this video possible through their affiliate program. I may earn a small commission if you buy through my link.

### Sponsored video

> This video is sponsored by [document-storage company]. I use their service to back up redacted records.

## 7. Compliance Review

Sentinel checks every video with a commercial relationship before publication.

## 8. Record Keeping

Keep records of:

- Sponsor or affiliate agreement date
- Product value
- Commission rate
- Approval rights
- Disclosure method used
- Published video URL
