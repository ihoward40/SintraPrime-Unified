# Appendix B — Universal Source Taxonomy (Normative)

## Derived From

BKGC Article V § 5.1 and Article VIII.

## Source Categories

| Category | Description | Default Weight |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Government Publication | Statutes, regulations, agency guidance, official records | Controlling within jurisdiction |
| Judicial Decision | Court opinions, orders, and published reasoning | Controlling or persuasive by level |
| Academic Scholarship | Peer-reviewed or scholarly analysis | Persuasive or scholarly |
| Historical Archive | Original historical records and documents | Historical |
| Commercial Publication | Professionally published treatises, manuals, guides | Persuasive or educational |
| Private Institutional Document | Internal manuals, policies, unpublished institutional materials | Requires provenance review |
| Independent Research | Publications by independent researchers | Emerging or scholarly |
| AI-Generated Output | Summaries, analyses, or reasoning produced by AI | Derivative; requires verification |
| Community Discussion | Forums, commentary, informal exchange | Educational or unverified |

## Rule

No category is accepted or rejected solely because of its origin. Default weights are rebuttable by provenance, corroboration, and jurisdictional relevance.

## Downstream Use

BKR `REGISTRIES/source_taxonomy.md` is the master registry. BRA implements the taxonomy in the Authority Engine.
