## 6. Redaction Checklist

Before sharing any document, remove or mask:

- [ ] Full Social Security number
- [ ] Full account numbers
- [ ] Full payment card or bank numbers
- [ ] Home address
- [ ] Dates of birth of minors
- [ ] Signatures
- [ ] Other sensitive identifiers
